# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free environment for everyone.

Examples of expected behavior:
- constructive and respectful communication
- empathy toward different viewpoints and experiences
- giving and receiving feedback professionally

Unacceptable behavior includes:
- harassment or personal attacks
- discriminatory language or conduct
- deliberate intimidation or disruption

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code of conduct.
They may remove or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this policy.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and public representations of the project.
